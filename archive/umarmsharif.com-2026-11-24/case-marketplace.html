The first 60 days: an incubation programme for new marketplace vendors

The problem

About 1 in 5 new vendors stayed inactive after onboarding.

The group target was to raise new-vendor activation from 80% to 90%. Incubation ownership was split across sales, account managers, agents and onboarding. Some entities ran about 10pp behind target.

Recommendation

Chase speed to first order, then route effort by vendor grade.

Early volume decides which vendors become large, and early volume depends on how fast the first order lands. The programme turns that into 4 decisions.

  • 01Chase speed to first order.
  • 02Route effort by vendor grade: automation for small vendors, people for high potential.
  • 03Set graduation thresholds by medians, market by market.
  • 04Measure it with a cascaded OKR tree.
