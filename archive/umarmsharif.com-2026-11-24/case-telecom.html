03 · Offer pitch analysis and experiments · Telenor

Which offers converted better: over-pitched or under-pitched.

Some offers were over-pitched and others under-pitched relative to what customers activated. The team needed to find the best-converting mix of validity, bundle and price.

The work done
Analysed offer presentations against activations by validity, bundle mix and price. Ran validity, price and sale-event experiments.
  • Under-pitched offers converted better.
  • 1-day bundles: 5.4% against 1.7% uptake, under-pitched against over-pitched.
  • 7-day bundles: 10.6% uptake (under-pitched).
  • Longer validity raised uptake by up to 1.3 points.
  • An 11.11 sale lifted activations by about 30%.

05 · Electricity failures and customer value, Karachi · Telenor

When outages hit, when are customers most reachable, and what do they use?

Outages hit sites unevenly. Marketing needed to know when customers were most reachable and what they used.

Method
Joined site-level outage logs to usage and ARPU. Clustered sites with matching outage timing within 5 km. Segmented every site into one of 26 towns.
  • About 42% of affected sites had daily outages.
  • Median outage days fell as ARPU rose.
  • Data and voice usage rose in outage hours, most for customers unused to outages.
  • A live World Cup super-over lifted outage-hour data by 20%.
  • Output: an outage-aware view for timing contextual offers.
