How I work

Look first. Then design, build, train and hand over.

The same sequence I used on the Urdu voice agent: find the step that costs the most, fix that one, and leave your team able to run it.

01

Look

I map how the work runs today, where time and money go, and which step is worth changing first.

02

Design

I define what the system does, what it must never do, and how we will know it works.

03

Build

I build a working version against real cases, then test it on written scenarios before anyone relies on it.

04

Train

Your team learns to use it, check it and improve it, with sessions built on their own work.

05

Hand over

You get the system, the documentation and the settings, running in your own accounts.

How I build a decision deck

A deck exists to move one decision. Everything else follows from that.

Strategy work usually ends in a board or leadership deck. I build every one the same way, and the argument is settled before a single slide is designed.

01

Frame the problem

Who reads it, what they already believe, and the one decision we want after the last slide. Every slide has to support that sentence.

02

Set the storyline

Pick how the argument opens: answer first for a busy board, or situation, complication, then answer when the room needs the tension.

03

Ghost deck

One row per slide: the headline finding and the layout, nothing more. If the headlines read top to bottom don't tell the story, we fix it here, before anything is built.

04

Write the slides

Each slide states its finding in the title, then proves it with one chart or diagram, a line of context and a named source.

05

Review

Read the titles alone. Check every claim against the evidence, one point of emphasis per slide, no filler words.

06

Deliver

An editable PowerPoint your team can keep using. The last slide is the ask or the next step.
